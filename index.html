What we do differently

Three common systems, applied with context.

Atlas draws from the method that fits the athlete, not the loudest training myth.

01

Bulgarian method

  • Daily maximal attempts and very high intensity.
  • Low variation with focus on a few core lifts.
  • Often favors a hip-dominant hinge pattern.
  • Relies on explosive hamstrings and glutes.
  • Values raw intensity and unusual resilience.
02

American approach

  • More exercise variation across the cycle.
  • Uses periodization over longer training blocks.
  • Balances volume and intensity more broadly.
  • Can work well for general development.
  • The technical thread can get diluted.
03

Chinese method

  • Highly technical and detail-driven training.
  • Uses accessory work and flexibility to shape positions.
  • Favors an upright torso and greater knee flexion.
  • Builds stronger quad contribution in the pull and receive.
  • Adapts well across different body types.

What Atlas uses

We use the Chinese method.

The snatch and clean and jerk reward precision, mobility, and repeatable positions. The Chinese method gives us a technical, adaptable system that can meet athletes where they are while still building strength for real attempts. It prioritizes technical efficiency over raw intensity.
