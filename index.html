Open gym

Bring your plan. Use the room.

You do not have to follow Atlas programming to train here. If you already work with an online coach, have your own training plan, or simply want to train on your own, open gym gives you access to the platforms and equipment without forcing a program on you.

Coached open gym support is available when you want eyes on a lift, but independent training stays welcome.
